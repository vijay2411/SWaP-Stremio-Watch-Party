SWaP runs only on https://web.stremio.com/* in the top-level page. It reads the current player's title, duration, playback position and buffering state to synchronize consenting room participants and warn about mismatched streams. It changes play/pause, seeking and playback rate, and adds the watch-party interface. It does not request access to other browsing sites, cookies or browsing-history APIs. Raw stream URLs and Stremio login credentials are not sent to room members.
